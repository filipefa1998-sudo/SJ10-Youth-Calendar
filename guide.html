🙌 Youth & Parents

No sign-in needed. Open the link and everything is there. You can look at anything — only leaders can change the calendar.

The next event with a live countdown, what’s coming up, and this month’s FSY invitation.

📅

Every activity, temple trip, camp and conference. Switch between Month, List and Year.

💌
Share

Love · Share · Invite ideas, your sharing journey, and easy invites for friends.

📚

Official Church links — 2027 youth theme, FSY guide, Come, Follow Me, temple appointments and more.

Finding what’s happening

  1. Tap a dayIts events show right below the calendar. A small dot under a date means something is planned.
  2. Tap an eventSee the time, place, who’s in charge and what to bring.
  3. Change monthsSwipe left or right on the calendar, or use the ‹ › arrows. Today jumps back to now.
  4. Filter or searchTap the colored chips to show only Young Women, Young Men, Temple, Camps… or type in the search box.

Add it to your home screen and it opens like a real app — with the sunrise temple icon.

  3. Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen”
  4. Tap “Add”The SJ10 Youth icon now lives on your home screen.

  2. Tap the ⋮ menuTop-right corner.
  3. Tap “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”

💌 Share the Gospel

The Share tab helps you live Love · Share · Invite — small, natural ways to share what you believe. No calling needed.

  1. Draw a mission cardTap the card for an idea. Pick 💛 Love, 💬 Share or 💌 Invite to choose the kind of idea, or leave it on All.
  2. Do it, then tap “✓ I did it!”It counts toward your monthly totals, your day streak and badges like 🕯️ First Light and 🔥 7-Day Streak.
  3. Invite a friendActivities marked 👋 Bring a friend show up here. Tap one to send a ready-made invite by text, WhatsApp or Messenger.
  4. Say it so friends get itFlip the word cards to learn friendlier ways to say Church words, like “Sunday worship service” instead of “sacrament meeting.”
🔒 Your sharing journey is private. It’s saved only on your own phone. Leaders can’t see it, and nothing is posted anywhere.

✏️ For Leaders

Leaders can post events, update the news banner, and set the monthly FSY invitation. Get the leader code from Unga — please don’t share it with youth.

Sign in

  1. Tap the 🔒 lockTop-right corner of the app.
  2. Enter the leader codeA gold bar appears showing you’re signed in.
  3. Type your name the first timeUse the same name every time (e.g. “Sis. Fa”). That name is how the app knows which events are yours.

Post an event

  1. Tap the gold ＋ buttonBottom-right — or tap a day on the calendar and use + Add.
  2. Fill in the detailsTitle, category (this sets the color), date, time and place. Add “what to bring” and who’s in charge — parents love that.
  3. Multi-day?Set an End date (camps, conferences).
  4. Repeats?Choose Every week or Monthly (same week & day) and a “Repeat until” date — e.g. every 1st Wednesday through June.
  5. Tap “Post event”It appears for everyone right away. 🎉

Edit or delete

  1. Tap your eventYou’ll see ✏️ Edit and Delete — only on events you posted.
  2. Repeating eventsWhen deleting, you can remove just one date or the whole series.

News banner & monthly invitation

  1. Tap “📣 News & invitation”In the gold leader bar.
  2. Update the scrolling newsReminders like “Permission slips due Sunday!”
  3. Set the FSY invitationPick the month and type the invitation the youth chose on fast Sunday.
✅ Good habits: post at least 2 weeks ahead · always add a time and place · use “Ward & Stake” or “Churchwide” only for those events · sign out on shared devices (🔒 → Sign out).

❓ Questions

Do I need an account or a password?

No. Anyone with the link can view everything. Only leaders use a code, and only to make changes.

Does “I’m in!” sign me up?

No — it’s just a fun celebration. For sign-ups, permission forms or rides, talk to your leader (check the event details for a sign-up link).

I don’t see a change a leader just made.

Changes show up within a few seconds. If not, pull down to refresh or close and reopen the app. Check that the footer says “Live calendar.”

What does the striped “Family Week” mean?

No youth activity that week — it’s time for family (holidays, General Conference, spring break).

The leader code isn’t working.
  • Check for extra spaces.
  • Make sure you’re using the leader code, not an old one.
  • Still stuck? Ask Unga.
I can’t edit an event.

Leaders can only edit events posted under the exact name they’re signed in with. Tap “Change name” in the gold bar to fix your name, or ask Unga — the master can edit anything.

I deleted something by mistake.

Ask Unga to restore it from the latest backup (👑 Master settings → Import backup), or just post it again.

Does anyone see what I log on the Share tab?

No. Your mission cards, streak and badges are saved only on your own device. If you switch phones or clear your browser, they start over.

Is this an official Church website?

No — it’s our ward’s youth calendar. All the Resources links go to official Church websites.
